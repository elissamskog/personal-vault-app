Privacy policy – personal vault

personal vault is used only by its developer, Elis Samskog, with a personal Google account. It accesses Google Calendar events and the calendar list solely to show and manage Elis's own calendar locally.

No data is collected from, shared with or sold to anyone. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the macOS Keychain. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
